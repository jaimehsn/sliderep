## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Summarized MAX line
The exercise breakdown SHALL show a MAX line's reps as "MAX", in the same place as any line's reps, joined with the interval's other reps by " + ". A MAX line's rep description and load SHALL show as a note, as on any line.

#### Scenario: Buy-in and MAX interval
- **WHEN** an own EMOM of every 3:00 has one interval of ROW 1 rep (200 m), BURPEES 8 and POWER SNATCHES MAX with load "60/40 kg"
- **THEN** the detail shows "ROW + BURPEES + POWER SNATCHES" with "200 m + 8 + MAX / interval" and the note "60/40 kg"
