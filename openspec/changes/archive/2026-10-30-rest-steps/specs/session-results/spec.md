## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Results with rest
A rested For Time's TIME SHALL include its rests, and the lap of a round's first split SHALL include the rest before it. A rep recorded during a rest SHALL NOT count in any score or split, with or without adjustments. Rests SHALL NOT be adjustable.

#### Scenario: Time with rests
- **WHEN** a 3-round For Time with 60 s of rest finishes its last rep at 6:40, after two rests
- **THEN** the score reads TIME 06:40

#### Scenario: Tabata score
- **WHEN** a Tabata of 8 intervals of AIR SQUATS 8 completes every interval with 70 reps in all
- **THEN** the score reads "8/8 · 70 reps"
